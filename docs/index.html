How we counted

Areas are the 12 ABS Statistical Areas Level 2 (suburb-sized areas) that make up the City of Maribyrnong and Hobsons Bay. Children are ABS estimates of residents aged 5-14.

A studio counts if it runs weekly or term classes for children aged about 5-14 at a venue inside the area. That includes calisthenics clubs and performing arts schools with dance classes. Holiday programs, one-off workshops, adult classes and theatre classes where dance is only one part do not count. Each studio counts once for each venue. We checked every studio against its own website, Instagram or council listing.

Treat the counts as a floor. A small studio with no website or social media could be missing, and children often cross area lines for classes.

Sources
